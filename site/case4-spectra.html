READING THESE RESULTS

Two normalizations. One explicit scope.

Coherent bin RMS · Pa
For the retained complex coefficient c, bin RMS = √2 |c|. The original periodic Hann transform was already divided by the sum of its weights. No second window is applied and no receiver trace is reconstructed here.
One-sided PSD · Pa²/Hz
PSD = 2 |c|² / ENBW = bin RMS² / ENBW. For these 4000 samples at 0.002 s cadence, ENBW = 0.1875 Hz, distinct from bin spacing Δf = 0.125 Hz. This is a propagated, single-record spectral-density estimate over the retained band, not a Welch average or a newly validated broadband stationary-noise estimate.
Band RMS and band level
The polar plots retain the original coherent band RMS. Its level is dB re 1 µPa, not a per-Hz level. The selected-band PSD sum × Δf equals coherent band mean square × (Δf / ENBW) = 2/3 of coherent band mean square. These two integrations must not be equated.
What this cannot establish
The observers are at 1 m, in the near-field for this low-frequency band. The strict-2D, span-invariant model represents ideal infinite-span geometry, not a finite turbine or resolved three-dimensional LES. Do not interpret these results as validated far-field sound levels, complete broadband noise, or evidence for a unique tonal source mechanism.
