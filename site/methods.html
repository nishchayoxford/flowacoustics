03 / SCHNERR–SAUER

Mass transfer changes the flow, not the acoustic kernel.

The installed OpenFOAM v2206 model returns a condensation coefficient Mc ≥ 0 and a signed vaporization coefficient Mv ≤ 0. Neither is the net source alone:

Both runs use psat = 2339.3 Pa, reference pressure 3500 Pa and surface tension 0.0728 N/m. Schnerr–Sauer supplies an algebraic mass-transfer closure; it does not integrate a Rayleigh–Plesset bubble trajectory, thermal bubble dynamics or collapse radiation. Phase change affects the reported acoustic contribution only through the recorded wall loading.

Installed coefficient law, nuclei and pressure regularization

Set a = min(max(α, 0), 1), Δp = p − psat, and ρb = aρℓ + (1 − a)ρv. The coefficient evaluation uses bounded a, while the transported α appears in the net-source equation.

04 / DISCRETIZATION & CLOSURE

What one time step actually does.

The matched pair each completed 160,000 fixed steps to nominally 10 s, on the same 26,751-cell mesh and two MPI ranks. Prescribed mesh motion and AMI interpolation update on the first PIMPLE outer iteration. Mesh-relative fluxes then enter the phase and momentum equations.

One extra non-orthogonal correction means two pressure passes. Thus eight physical-pressure matrix solves occur per time step, plus separate mesh-flux pcorr solves—not eight time advances. Only the final non-orthogonal pass updates flux and velocity. The final-pressure tolerance applies to the last pressure pass in each outer iteration; no residual-based outer-loop early exit is configured. The second MULES correction is weighted by one half; zero artificial interface compression does not disable boundedness correction.

maxCo=0.5 is not an enforced cap because adjustTimeStep=no. Recorded maximum fluid Courant numbers are approximately 0.717741 (ON) and 0.825344 (OFF). Linear-solver tolerances are configured stopping criteria, not a claim that every solve attained them.

WALE is algebraic, not a k–ω solve

The matched runs update WALE only on the final outer iteration. It computes viscosity from the local velocity gradient; it does not transport k and ω. In the two-dimensional cubeRootVol branch, filter width uses cell volume V divided by extrusion thickness h:

The original URANS cases instead use kOmegaSST, transporting turbulent kinetic energy and specific dissipation rate ωSST. This ωSST is not the acoustic angular frequency ω = 2πf used below. Strict-2D WALE does not resolve three-dimensional turbulence.

05 / TWO SEPARATE WORKFLOWS

Advance the flow. Then propagate the sources.

CFD recording is the handoff between these workflows. The acoustic calculation is one-way postprocessing; it does not feed receiver pressure back into PIMPLE.

06 / THE CFD–ACOUSTIC HANDOFF

Specify the source before comparing sound.

A closed contour C(t) bounds the excluded region. Its normal n points out of that region into the surrounding fluid. In the general FW-H surface definitions, U is inertial fluid velocity, v is actual surface velocity, and the subscript n denotes a normal component.

General surface definitions before implementation choices

Original four cases / both implemented surface terms

For exact no-penetration on a moving body, Un = vn: relative momentum flux vanishes but Q = ρ₀vn remains as thickness sound. On the fixed permeable recording envelope, v = 0; it is not a porous wall. Body and envelope estimates overlap: do not sum them or interpret their difference as an isolated quadrupole.

Omitting exterior quadrupoles can produce spurious signals in permeable-surface calculations; this is a known limitation, not proof that the present result is validated.[3]

Matched ON/OFF / pressure loading only

Actual orbit-plus-pitch positions, normals, fluid velocity and surface motion remain intact. Thickness, relative advective loading, viscous traction, cavity-volume radiation, bubble-collapse sound and exterior quadrupoles are absent from this matched contribution.

Normals, measure and force signs

At solid walls, OpenFOAM’s fluid-out face-area vector Sf is reversed to obtain the body-out normal: n = −Sf/|Sf|. The line measure is ds = |Sf|/dextrusion. Remove numerical extrusion depth once; integrate all panels once, with no extra blade-count multiplier. The matched adapter uses first-frame line lengths and separately audits their tiny raw variation.

Pressure force on the solid is −∮pgn ds, in N/m of invariant span, opposite in sign to integrated pressure loading L. A permeable-contour pressure integral is not solid-body force. Receiver pressure is Pa, not Pa/m.

07 / PROPAGATION, NOT A 3D FARASSAT KERNEL

A two-dimensional Green function.

The custom FW-H-derived implementation uses forward Fourier sign exp(−iωt) and inverse sign exp(+iωt). With R the source–receiver distance and k = ω/c₀, its outgoing free-space Green function is

The Fourier transform means â(ω) = ∫a(t)exp(−iωt)dt; inversion is (1/2π)∫â(ω)exp(+iωt)dω. Transforming the whole moving distribution retains motion terms without an extra guessed Doppler factor. Both reactive and radiating cylindrical content are retained; this is not a far-field-only or stationary-force approximation.

This is not a call to libAcoustics or a three-dimensional Farassat implementation. Homogeneous-water c₀ = 1480 m/s and zero acoustic mean flow are propagation assumptions. Refraction and scattering by vapour are not included.

Fast evaluation without discarding time samples

The reference path evaluates the Hankel kernel at each moving position. Production moving-geometry evaluation uses six terms of its convergent logarithmic Bessel expansion only when the maximum actual kR ≤ 1. Fixed geometry instead permits source FFTs followed by fixed-contour integration. NIST gives the underlying Jν power-series and Y₀ logarithmic-series definitions.[1][2]

Outside the moving-series domain the reducer fails rather than silently launching unrestricted direct evaluation. Spatial panel partitions retain every time sample in the selected window; signed complex coefficients and absolute truncation bounds are added across partitions. This is not a compact-force replacement. The bound covers series truncation relative to the same sampled integral, not CFD, missing physics or quadrature error.

08 / FINITE-RECORD ESTIMATOR

What a receiver history means.

For N retained samples, endpoint-fit cadence is Δt = (tlast − tfirst)/(N − 1), period T = NΔt and positive frequencies fm = m/T. The endpoint is not repeated. Only bins inside the requested band and strictly below Nyquist are retained. Let Sm,j denote the moving contour sum in the preceding integral, evaluated at angular frequency ωm and sample j.

Remove the Eulerian kernel-projected mean before tapering—not the mean of each moving material panel. A steady blade-attached load can create a time-varying receiver field. The fast series applies the same linear operation to separable time moments. Hann coherent gain is 0.5, RMS gain is √(3/8), and equivalent noise bandwidth is 1.5/T.

The five-cycle lower edge is an estimator rule, not proof of useful acoustic bandwidth or stationarity. The late window changes both duration and band: its difference is sensitivity, not an uncertainty interval.

Periodic/window-conditioned, band-limited pressure: these are not causal first-arrival histories. Two-dimensional propagation has an interior light-cone tail, so a travel-time cutoff cannot remove unknown prehistory. Source-force plots instead retain the complete untapered record. Exact zero levels are represented by null/status, not an artificial pressure floor. ON−OFF level difference, difference of RMS magnitudes and RMS of the signed pressure difference are different quantities.

Native timestamps and the two cadence roles

All actual timestamps are retained and the binary64 fixed-step recurrence is audited. No nominal clock substitution or interpolation is performed. The full-record endpoint fit defines the analysis-plan cadence, period, frequency-spacing/ENBW/Nyquist metadata and requested lower edge; each retained window’s endpoint fit determines its exported frequency array. FFT index phase differs slightly from raw-time phase; this approximation has a separately recorded bound. Synthesis uses the actual timestamps. Download the clock audit.

09 / EVIDENCE & LIMITS

Numerical acceptance is not physical validation.

The matched recordings contain 5000 actual source times on 546 body panels per run. An independent reference decodes hash-checked native binary records, reconstructs pressure loading from primitive absolute pressure and geometry, and evaluates a distinct Chebyshev J/Y expansion with 32 and 64 nodes. It covers every source time and panel in both windows at all twelve receivers. Selected-frequency literal Hankel checks are additional controls, not an all-bin direct evaluation.

The retained acceptance record compares signed complex coefficients, actual-time synthesized pressure, force, RMS and serialization; repeated offline exports were byte-identical. These are results of the completed numerical acceptance, not new tests or simulations performed for this page. Analytical signed monopole, dipole and moving-source controls test the acoustic machinery separately from the CFD.

Interpretation boundary. The twelve receivers at 1 m are acoustically near-field in these bands. The model assumes infinite span and quiescent free-space water. There is no finite-span correction, experimental validation, established mesh/time convergence, demonstrated stationarity or demonstrated useful acoustic bandwidth. Lower selected pressure-loading levels with phase change ON do not establish lower total cavitation noise.

Native-field evidence takes precedence over visualization sampling: the OFF phase planes are not admissible evidence of vapour production. Its retained native endpoint liquid fraction differs from unity only at roundoff level.
